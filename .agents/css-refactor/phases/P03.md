## Watch out

`PageHeading` already exists as a real Super Admin component
(`src/features/super-admin/components/PageHeading.tsx`) — each page renders it
itself as the first thing in its content, because the chrome deliberately stopped
taking `title`/`subtitle`/`action` props. Preserve that arrangement; this phase
moves the component into the shared library, it does not re-plumb it into the
shell.

`Notice` feeds `AccessNotices.tsx` (`AccessBlockedScreen`, `PaymentWarningBanner`),
which is auth-adjacent. Do not alter its copy, its owner/employee split, or the
conditions under which it renders.
